If you found your way here, you're probably either thinking about buying a home, refinancing one you already own, or trying to help a buyer who is. Either way — welcome. This blog exists for one reason: to help you make a more confident decision about the biggest loan of your life.

## What you'll find here

Plain-English posts on:

- How pre-approval actually works (and what to ask before you let anyone pull your credit).
- The trade-offs between conventional, FHA, VA, and investment loans — written for normal humans.
- What's actually moving in the market and what it means for your monthly payment.
- Tactical tips for real estate agents who want their files to close on time.

## What you won't find here

Hype. "Lowest rate guaranteed!" claims. Doomscrolling about rates. I'd rather walk you through the math once than write five posts trying to scare you into a phone call.

If you have a question you'd like me to write about, send it over from the [contact page](/contact). The best posts on this site will come from real questions real buyers ask.

— Keith
